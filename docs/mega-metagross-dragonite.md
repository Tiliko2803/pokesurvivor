# Mega Metagross and Mega Dragonite — approved Live release

The approved /dev/ mechanics and packed atlases are promoted to production. Both existing families gain standard Iridium evolution (45 total family copies, three beyond complete Gold), resale/save support and the shared Mega animator. Survivor stones unlock with three bosses in one clean ordinary solo run with the base form. Tests retain unlocked stones and isolated guest saves.

Mega Metagross starts Meteor Mash level 2 with Steel/Psychic stats 80/145/150/105/110/110. Cruz Meteórica fires four physical Steel rays every six seconds (240 × 48 before area modifiers; base 38 + 2.5 × level). In TFT, its four cardinal range-three strikes deal 200% attack, stun 0.6 seconds (bosses half) and grant a 20% max HP self-shield on contact, within the existing 50% shield cap. Ordinary Metagross retains its lift/throw behavior.

Mega Dragonite starts Dragon Breath level 2 with custom fork multipliers 1.10 HP, 1.22 attack and 1.08 speed. Pulso del Guardián fires a special Dragon ray every eight seconds (440 × 60 before area modifiers; base 65 + 3 × level), healing 4% max HP once on contact. In TFT, the cardinal range-five beam deals 260% attack; contact heals the two most injured living allies within radius two by 120% attack each, subject to antiheal. Missing does not heal; disabled/dead casters cannot activate it.

Both passives start after 1.5 seconds, use combat time, respect walls and stop while dead/remote/frozen. No new transient save fields. Atlases are 880×880, 176px cells, foot anchor (88,164). Five source facings and mirrored directions; idle/walk/windup/release poses, recoil/fade hurt and faint presentation. No dedicated shiny sheets. Dragonite rear-left release reuses a correctly facing idle pose and generated alpha speckles are cleaned in packing.

Validation: 204 automated mechanics/production/preview tests; script parsing and root asset path/dimension checks. Browser QA was not rerun because Chromium is unavailable. Atlas bytes and published entry version are checked after deployment.
